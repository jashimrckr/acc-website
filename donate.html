Key Focus Areas

What the Fund Supports

Contributions of any amount are appreciated and will directly advance high-leverage strategic initiatives that strengthen overlooked and underinvested communities:

  • Leadership and career development for rising changemakers
  • Entrepreneurial innovation and inclusive ecosystem building
  • Technology for lending, risk management, secondary market access, and impact reporting.
  • Storytelling that showcases the resilience and ingenuity of Appalachian communities
  • Equity gaps in project capital stacks and technical assistance in underinvested places
