2026 CDFI Ecosystem Intelligence

Member Survey Findings • 2026

Insights from 38 responding institutions across ACC's 46-member network highlight the vital role of community-based lenders in supporting small businesses, revitalizing local economies, and expanding access to capital across Appalachia.

71%

Provide small-business microloans under $50,000, expanding access to financing for local entrepreneurs.

76%

Offer business loans exceeding $100,000, supporting business growth and expansion.

61%

Finance commercial real estate, supporting investment in downtowns, business properties, and community revitalization.

39%

Finance community facilities that support essential local services and infrastructure.

51%

Anticipate needing at least $5 million in additional capital over the next 12–14 months to meet lending demand.

67%

Identify the cost of capital as one of their greatest organizational challenges.

Long-Term Strategic Horizon

ACC Vision 2030: Mobilizing $250 Million

By 2030, ACC plans to mobilize $250M in catalytic capital to expand economic opportunity, grow resilient local businesses, create family-sustaining jobs, and build lasting community wealth across Appalachia.

$7M Strategic Investment Ask

To achieve this vision, ACC is securing $7M in targeted catalytic investments:

  • $2M Unrestricted operating capital to reinforce leadership, data infrastructure, and lending technology.
  • $5M Equity balance sheet capital, leveraging additional debt capital by a factor of nine (9:1) for regional members.
