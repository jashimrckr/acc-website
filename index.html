Building Opportunity
across Appalachia

Appalachia has extraordinary assets: entrepreneurial talent, abundant natural resources, strong local leadership, and deeply rooted financial institutions.

Lasting progress depends on more than how much capital reaches the region.

It matters who puts that capital to work, how decisions are made, and whether communities have the resources and capacity to shape their own futures.

For sustainable progress, what matters is not only how much capital reaches the region, but also who deploys it, how decisions are made, and whether communities are positioned to build lasting local wealth.

Rooted in communities.
Connected for greater impact.

Appalachian Community Capital (ACC) is a nonprofit Community Development Financial Institution working across Appalachia and other underinvested rural communities.

At the heart of ACC is a network of 46 community-based CDFIs with more than $5 billion in combined assets. These institutions know their communities—the entrepreneurs, projects, opportunities, and challenges affecting local economies.

ACC connects this local knowledge and leadership with capital, expertise, partners, and shared resources—creating opportunities and scale that no single institution can achieve alone.

The result is a powerful model: decisions stay close to communities while a regional network creates the scale to do more.
