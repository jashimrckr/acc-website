**Appalachian Community Capital (ACC) is a nonprofit Community Development Financial Institution working to expand economic opportunity and strengthen locally rooted institutions across Appalachia.** 

Since **2013, ACC has grown from an innovative approach to increasing the flow of capital into the region into a network and platform for collaboration, investment, and innovation across Appalachia and other underinvested rural communities.**

### **Our Mission** 

ACC’s mission is to increase small business lending by providing Appalachian member institutions that serve underserved people and communities with new sources of capital. ACC is committed to providing reliable and sufficient capital for all its members, leading to a growing economy, increased entrepreneurship, higher quality jobs, and increased local wealth in the Appalachian region. 

### **Our Core Strategy: Place-Led. Networked for Scale.** 

ACC believes lasting economic progress is strongest when resources, leadership, and long-term decision-making remain rooted in communities. 

We connect locally rooted institutions to capital, partners, expertise, and shared resources that expand their reach and impact—combining local knowledge and leadership with the strength of a regional network. 

### **History** 

ACC was founded by the Appalachian Regional Commission and its partners to address a persistent challenge: community lenders across Appalachia needed greater access to capital, but, individually, often lacked the scale to efficiently reach larger sources of investment. 

ACC offered a new approach—bringing together the capital needs of community lenders across the region, attracting investment at greater scale, and creating a simpler pathway for investors seeking to put capital to work in Appalachia. 

With $15.5 million in seed capital, ACC began operations in 2015. Since then, ACC has raised an additional $40 million for its members, supporting financing for 251 small businesses and help create or retain almost 3,000 jobs. 

As the needs and opportunities facing rural communities have evolved, so has ACC. In 2023, ACC expanded its work nationally to support financing for low- and zero-emission products, technologies and services, creating a national membership for aligned organizations serving energy communities. 

Today, ACC’s work extends beyond its original role of increasing access to capital. ACC brings together community lenders, investors, public and philanthropic partners, and other expertise, to strengthen local institutions, develop new financing approaches, and pursue opportunities that can have impact across the region.

## **4. WHAT We Do** 

### **What We Do** 

ACC works across capital, capacity and collaboration to strengthen locally rooted institutions and expand economic opportunity across Appalachia. 

##### **Expand access to capital** 

ACC brings new sources and types of capital into the region and creates pathways for community lenders to put those resources to work. 

Our approach includes lending and loan participations, Community Investment Notes, and financing partnerships with banks, foundations, impact investors, and other capital partners. We also develop new capital strategies in areas of emerging opportunity, including energy and climate finance and employee ownership. 

##### **Strengthen community lenders** 

Strong local institutions are essential to strong local economies. ACC helps members build capacity, share knowledge, and work together more effectively. 

That includes technical assistance, peer learning, leadership development, shared services and collaborative solutions, and data and analytics that help members make decisions and deploy capital more effectively. 

##### **Build solutions together** 

Some opportunities and challenges require institutions to work across organizations, sectors, and geographies. ACC brings together community lenders, capital providers, public and philanthropic partners, and other expertise to develop and test new approaches. 

Initiatives such as Opportunity Appalachia and Green Bank for Rural America demonstrate how ACC can use the strength of its network and partnerships to move projects forward, develop new financing approaches, and respond to emerging regional needs.
