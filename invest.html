INVEST IN ACC THROUGH OUR COMMUNITY INVESTMENT NOTES

Accessible, mission-aligned fixed income investment offering for accredited investors

Private investment opportunity for mission-aligned investors, moving capital efficiently to the front lines of community-driven economic development.

Terms & profile:

  • Stable, fixed-income returns directly aligned to measurable social and environmental benefit.
  • Term: 1–10 years.
  • Rates: 0–3.50%.
  • Investor profile: Accredited individuals, trusts, faith-based organizations, foundations, and mission-aligned institutions.
  • Use of proceeds: Intermediary capital deployed through ACC’s CDFI network to finance small businesses, energy infrastructure, and community facilities.
